## Description

Waylaid Value helps you compare Waylaid Crates and estimate the gold needed to unlock and craft Merchant's Favor rewards in WoW Forever.

### Crates

See the cheapest priced filling bundle for each crate, the Favor it gives, the total net cost and cost per Favor. Select a crate to compare all accepted filling options. Missing prices remain visible.

### Rewards

Start with your character's professions, or browse other professions. Combine search, armor type, level/class, role relevance and crafting-skill filters. For my class keeps physical DPS/general supplies for hunters and leaves unclassified items visible; All roles restores the wider list. Select a reward to see its recipe Favor cost, an exact whole-crate shopping plan, crafting materials and estimated total gold. Enter Favor already held or mark an already learned recipe to remove costs you no longer need to pay. Item names/icons follow rarity and money units use gold/silver/copper colors.

### Prices

Reads cached TSM minimum-buyout prices or a compatible Auctionator v1 API. Auto can use both. Install and configure TSM with AppHelper, or a compatible Auctionator build, to see price estimates. OneForAll is not required.

Refresh recalculates existing cached prices; it does not scan the AH. Quotes do not guarantee available quantities. Turn-in silver uses the documented calculation assumption of 50 copper per base Favor, excluding first-turn-in/random bonuses. Provisional Expert rewards are opt-in; incomplete estimates are labelled.

### Open the addon

Type `/crates` or `/waylaid`, or click the crate icon on the minimap. Drag the minimap icon to reposition it.

This addon does not buy, craft, scan the auction house or turn in crates automatically.
