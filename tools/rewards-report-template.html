Pricing assumptions, sources and data license

Net = the cost of whole crates and their selected bundles, less documented turn-in silver, plus the materials for one craft. Existing Favor is treated as already paid for. Recipe unlock is charged once. Net uses the documented 0.5s returned per base Favor; confirm the quest reward in game. Upfront cost and leftover Favor appear in each item’s details.

The planner uses documented Apprentice/Journeyman rewards only, respects the player level, and permits mixed crates and overshoot. Unit minimum prices × quantities are estimates: stock and stack costs at those prices are unverified. No first-turn-in or random bonus is included.

Materials are bought directly at AH prices. No vendor reagent prices, nested crafting, existing reagent inventory, profession levelling, reputation acquisition, crafting fees, or finished-item purchase comparison. Costs are per craft, not per output unit; consumable yields are unverified.

“Armor for class” filters body armor by class, item level requirement, and the mail/plate proficiency available at level 40. It excludes weapons, accessories, enchants and non-armor rewards; it does not assess stats, trained skills, reputation, or whether you know the recipe. All classes / all rewards shows the full catalogue.
