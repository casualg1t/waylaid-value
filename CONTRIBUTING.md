# Contributing

Open an issue with the addon version, price provider and reproducible steps. Do not upload credentials, raw SavedVariables or account details. Redact screenshots.

Run `python3 tools/check.py` before proposing changes. Keep Lua compatible with 5.1. Unknown data must remain explicit; do not replace missing buyout prices with guessed market prices. Avoid adding purchase/crafting automation. Preserve third-party attribution when updating recipe data.
